RESPECT OWNERSHIP AND REPORT PROBLEMS

Copyright & Content Reports

How to report content, protect creative work, and request review when material may violate someone’s rights.

Effective July 30, 2026

Respect creators

Users may post only content they created, have permission to use, or otherwise have a lawful basis to share. Khmer Together may remove or restrict content when credible information indicates that it infringes copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights.

Report content inside Khmer Together

  1. Open the post.
  2. Tap Report.
  3. Select the closest reason and explain what is wrong.
  4. Include enough information for the administrator to locate and evaluate the content.

The built-in report system is the current channel for ordinary community complaints.

Copyright notice information

A formal copyright claim should normally identify:

  • The copyrighted work claimed to be infringed.
  • The exact Khmer Together content or location at issue.
  • Your name and reliable contact information.
  • A good-faith statement that the disputed use is not authorized by the owner, its agent, or the law.
  • A statement that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act for the rights holder.
  • Your physical or electronic signature.

Counter-notices and mistakes

If your content is removed because of a copyright complaint and you believe the removal was a mistake or misidentification, preserve your evidence and request review. A formal counter-notice process and designated contact information will be published after Khmer Together establishes its legal operating identity and public copyright contact channel.

Repeat infringement

Khmer Together may restrict or terminate accounts that repeatedly post infringing material when appropriate under the circumstances.

Important launch-stage limitation: Khmer Together has not yet published or claimed a registered DMCA designated agent on this page. Before broad public promotion, the operator should establish the correct legal identity, public contact information, and—after legal review—consider registering a designated agent with the U.S. Copyright Office.

Other rights

Use the Report button for impersonation, privacy violations, unauthorized intimate material, scams, harassment, or trademark concerns. Explain the issue clearly and provide links or screenshots when available.

False reports

Do not knowingly submit false claims. Misrepresentations may harm users and may carry legal consequences.

This page provides operational guidance and does not determine whether a use is legally authorized, fair use, or infringing. Those questions can require fact-specific legal analysis.
