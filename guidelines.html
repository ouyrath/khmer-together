Community Guidelines

The behavioral and content standards that help Khmer Together remain welcoming, useful, and safe.

Effective July 30, 2026

Our standard

Khmer Together exists to help Khmer people connect, celebrate culture, support one another, share opportunities, and build a strong community. Speak to others as you would want your family to be treated.

Be authentic

  • Use an identity that honestly represents you, your organization, or your business.
  • Do not impersonate people, misrepresent affiliations, or operate deceptive accounts.
  • Clearly disclose paid promotions, business relationships, and material conflicts of interest.

Be respectful

  • No bullying, harassment, stalking, threats, humiliation, or unwanted sexual attention.
  • No attacks based on race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, caste, sex, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or serious disease.
  • Debate ideas without dehumanizing people.

Protect safety and privacy

  • Do not encourage violence, self-harm, dangerous challenges, or criminal activity.
  • Do not publish another person’s private address, phone number, financial data, passwords, identification, or intimate material without permission.
  • Never exploit, sexualize, groom, or endanger minors.

Use private messages responsibly

  • Do not use one-to-one or group messages for repeated unwanted contact, threats, harassment, sexual exploitation, non-consensual intimate photos or recordings, illegal media, or pressure for money or personal information.
  • Respect a person who asks you to stop contacting them.
  • Block and report suspicious or unsafe conversations. Conversation reports do not automatically include private message contents.

No scams or manipulation

  • No fake jobs, fake investments, advance-fee schemes, phishing, pyramid schemes, or fraudulent fundraising.
  • No malware, harmful downloads, coordinated spam, fake engagement, or misleading links.
  • Business offers should state important terms honestly and clearly.

Respect intellectual property

  • Post your own work or content you have permission or a lawful basis to use.
  • Give credit when appropriate, but understand that credit alone does not replace permission.
  • Do not remove watermarks or falsely claim someone else’s work.

Restricted content

Content may be removed or limited when it includes graphic violence, sexual content, regulated goods, illegal services, dangerous misinformation, or other material that creates significant risk. Context such as news reporting, education, or public-interest discussion may be considered but does not guarantee that content will remain.

Enforcement

Possible actions include warnings, reduced visibility, content removal, feature restrictions, temporary suspension, permanent account removal, preservation of evidence, and referral to appropriate authorities. Severity, context, history, intent, and risk may affect the response.

Reporting

Use the Report button on a post when content may violate these rules. Reports are private from the reported user. Do not abuse reporting tools to target people you disagree with.

Immediate danger: Khmer Together is not an emergency service. Contact local emergency services or an appropriate trusted authority when someone faces immediate danger.

Private group responsibilities

  • Group owners and admins should add only appropriate members and remove members who create safety risks.
  • Do not add someone repeatedly after they leave, or use groups to bypass a block.
  • Private does not mean consequence-free. Members can report unsafe groups, members, and content.
