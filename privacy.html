HOW INFORMATION IS HANDLED

Privacy Policy

What Khmer Together collects, why it is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to community members.

Effective July 30, 2026

1. Information we collect

Account information

Email address, authentication identifiers, display name, username, profile photo, and bio.

Community activity

Posts, photos, comments, likes, follows, reports, notifications, private conversations, private messages, read status, and related timestamps.

Technical information

Device, browser, network, security, log, and approximate location information that may be processed by hosting and authentication providers.

Communications

Information you provide in reports, support requests, or other communications.

2. How we use information

  • Provide authentication, profiles, feeds, posting, photos, comments, likes, following, notifications, private messaging, and moderation.
  • Protect users, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and secure accounts and infrastructure.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, analyze, and improve Khmer Together.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms and Community Guidelines.
  • Communicate about accounts, safety, policy changes, and service operations.

3. Service providers

Khmer Together uses Supabase for authentication, database, and file storage, and Vercel for website hosting and delivery. Those providers process information as needed to supply their services. Additional providers may be added as Khmer Together grows, and this policy will be updated when appropriate.

4. Public information

Your display name, username, profile details, posts, photos, comments, likes, and follow relationships may be visible to authenticated community members. Do not post sensitive personal information, financial credentials, government identification numbers, private addresses, or anything you do not want others to see.

5. Private messages

Private messages are intended to be readable only by the two conversation participants through the normal service interface. Administrators do not automatically receive message contents when a conversation is reported. Message records are stored by our infrastructure provider and may be accessed or preserved when required by law, necessary to investigate a serious safety or security issue, or needed to operate and protect the service.

Do not use private messages to send passwords, financial credentials, government identification numbers, medical records, or other highly sensitive information.

6. Cookies and local storage

Authentication tools may use browser storage, cookies, or similar technologies to keep you signed in, protect sessions, and operate essential features. Khmer Together currently does not use behavioral advertising cookies.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers, when you direct us to share it, to investigate abuse, to protect rights and safety, in connection with a lawful business transfer, or when required by law.

8. Retention

Information is retained while your account is active and as reasonably needed to operate the service, resolve disputes, enforce rules, meet legal requirements, preserve safety evidence, and maintain backups. Deleted content may remain temporarily in caches or backups.

9. Your choices

  • Edit your profile and your own comments using available controls.
  • Delete your own posts, comments, and sent private messages using available controls.
  • Unfollow users and report content.
  • Request account or data assistance through the support channel once published.

Some requests may require identity verification and may be limited by safety, fraud-prevention, recordkeeping, or legal obligations.

10. Children

Khmer Together is not directed to children under 13, and users under 13 may not create accounts. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13 without appropriate authorization, we will take reasonable steps to remove it.

11. Security

We use access controls, database security policies, authentication, and reputable infrastructure providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your password and notify the platform if you suspect unauthorized account access.

12. International use

Khmer Together is designed to connect Khmer people in different countries. Information may be processed in the United States or other locations where service providers operate, which may have different data-protection laws.

13. Policy changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as features, providers, or legal obligations change. Material changes will be communicated through the service or another reasonable method.

14. Contact

A dedicated privacy contact channel will be published before wider public promotion. Until then, do not submit sensitive documents through posts or comments.

This starter policy should be reviewed by a qualified professional before wider public promotion, especially when advertising, analytics, payments, location services, or a custom support address is added.
